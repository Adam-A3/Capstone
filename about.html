About

Our story and what we believe

Adam's Robots is named after our founder, Adam. He had an idea of creating functional humanoid robots and wanted to bring that idea to life. He and a group of friends built their first prototype robot (pictured). After successfully building a functional prototype bot, Adam and the team continued experimenting and improving their designs. After perfecting the designs, the robots were ready to be sold to the public under the brand Adam's Robots.

What we believe

Quality

All of our products inspected and tested to make sure they meet our high standards. Our robots are of the highest quality.

Innovation

We are always researching and looking for new ways to make our robots even greater. Innovation is a core part of who we are
